Advertised plan comparison · Frontier deals

  • Speed and data: Frontier advertises unlimited data with no overage charges. Compare the plan’s listed upload and download speeds with the customer’s current plan. Wired plan speeds and Wi-Fi speeds on a particular device are different. Frontier lists 500 Mbps, 1 Gig, 2 Gig, and 5 Gig plans, and advertises 7 Gig in select areas. Fiber overview · 7 Gig availability
  • Price and promotions: Use the address quote and Broadband Facts label. Confirm the regular and promotional prices, when the promotion ends, Auto Pay and paperless billing requirements, installation charges, add-ons, and any contract or cancellation terms. Confirm gift card or other reward eligibility and redemption steps before quoting it. Broadband Facts labels
  • Router and Wi-Fi: Frontier says its internet plans include a router with no monthly equipment rental fee. Its Whole-Home Wi-Fi page advertises inclusion on Fiber 1 Gig or faster plans. Check the specific quote for the equipment, coverage options, and any charges. Whole-Home Wi-Fi
  • Installation and switching: Some properties need a fiber line run from the street before installation. Check the route and any required permissions with Frontier. When practical, test the new service before canceling the old one. Installation guide
  • TV, home phone, and work needs: Confirm TV subscription cost and channels separately. Frontier Fiber Digital Voice runs over the fiber network; confirm availability, number transfer, backup power, and compatibility before changing phone service. Verify static IP or equipment requirements with Frontier before committing. Fiber Digital Voice
